Frequently Asked Questions

Is this app 100% free and open source? ▼

Yes, Trace is 100% free and licensed under the MIT License. You can inspect the complete Rust source code, build your own APKs, and export your data anytime.

Does recording continue when the screen turns off? ▼

Yes! Trace implements both the Screen WakeLock API and a background Web Audio oscillator keep-alive engine. This prevents Android from putting the location acquisition thread to sleep during long runs, drives, or bike tours.

Can I import the GPX files into Strava, Garmin, or Google Earth? ▼

Absolutely. The exported `.gpx` files strictly follow the GPX 1.1 specification and include elevation, ISO-8601 timestamps, and extended telemetry tags for instant compatibility with Strava, Garmin Connect, Google Earth, and QGIS.
